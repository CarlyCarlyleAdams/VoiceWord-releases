# VoiceWord

**Speak when you want to write. Listen when you want to read.**

VoiceWord is a free Windows app that lets you use your computer by talking and listening, not just reading and typing. Hold a key and talk, and your words are typed wherever your cursor is. Highlight some text, or a whole page, and VoiceWord reads it to you. It can also check your writing and explain words. It runs on your own PC, so what you say stays private.

## Download

Get the newest version here: https://github.com/CarlyCarlyleAdams/VoiceWord-releases/releases/latest

Download the file that ends in .exe and run it. VoiceWord works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Once it is installed, it tells you when a new version is ready.

## Support and feedback

VoiceWord is free. If it helps you, you can support it at https://ko-fi.com/voicewordapp

Got an idea, or something isn't working? In VoiceWord's tray menu choose Help, then Send feedback or an idea. Or email voicewordapp@gmail.com
